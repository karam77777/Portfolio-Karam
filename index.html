About Me

WHO I AM

I am Karam Shama Sayed, a passionate Graphic Designer based in Giza, Egypt, with over 10 years of professional experience spanning print, digital, branding, and advertising design.

My expertise covers Print Design, Visual Identity, Social Media, Brochure and Book Design, Package Design, and Advertising Campaigns. I have managed entire design departments and personally trained over 20 designers.

Fully proficient in the Adobe Creative Suite including Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Acrobat Pro, and CapCut Pro, from concept to final print-ready delivery.

Beyond design, I bring strong computer hardware expertise including assembly, maintenance, and troubleshooting, making me a uniquely versatile professional.
